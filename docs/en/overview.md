# Podcast Widget

The Podcast Widget displays a player for **one** podcast episode on a
page. Readers can listen to the episode right there, without having to
navigate to the podcast section.

## Two Modes

- **Latest Episode:** The widget always displays the most recent episode of the
  selected podcast. When a new episode is released, the widget updates
  automatically—no changes to the page are required. Ideal for homepages,
  podcast landing pages, and channel pages.
- **Specific Episode:** The widget permanently displays only the selected
  episode. Ideal for posts or pages that refer to a specific episode
  .

## Two Player Sizes

- **Large:** Cover image, title, date, play/pause, progress bar with
  timestamps, playback speed (1× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 2×), and
  mute button. For wide sections and pages where the podcast is the
  main focus.
- **Compact:** Narrow tile with cover image, title, date and time, Play/Pause
  and a thin progress bar. No playback speed or
  mute control. For narrow columns and overviews.

## Important to Know

- The podcast must already exist in Staffbase and have at least one episode
 . The widget does not create podcasts or episodes.
- It always plays **one** episode, never a list of episodes.
- There is no autoplay, and the playback position is not
  saved: When you reopen the page, the episode starts over from
  the beginning.
