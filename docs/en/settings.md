# Settings

Everything is configured in the **Select Podcast** window. The settings dialog
itself contains only the button that opens it.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Podcasts (list on the left) | The podcast from which an episode is displayed. Required. The arrow next to a podcast loads its episodes; clicking an episode selects it and sets the display to `Specific Episode`. The search field above filters podcasts and episodes by title. The selected podcast appears in the center as a heading. |
| Display – Which Episode | `Latest Episode` (default) always shows the most recent episode. `Specific Episode` permanently displays the episode selected on the left; it appears below under **Episode**. |
| Player – Size | `Large` (default): Cover art, title, date, full controls including playback speed and mute. `Compact`: narrow tile with play and progress. |
| Enter ID manually | Only necessary if the list does not load: Enter the podcast or episode ID, or the full URL. |

## Notes

- **Apply** is only available once a podcast has been selected—and in
  `Specific Episode` mode, an episode as well. Otherwise, the status bar at the top will indicate
  what is missing.
- **Apply** saves the changes to the settings dialog; they are not saved until you
  click **Save** there.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile devices display the content exactly as it appears on the page.
  The episode is loaded from Staffbase; it cannot be played in the
  preview. The display and player are located on the right under
  **Settings**.
- In `Latest Episode` mode, a selected episode remains saved but is
  ignored—it reappears when you switch back.
- Changing the player size only affects the display, not the
  episode being played.
- Undo and Redo are located at the top of the window (⌘/Ctrl Z and ⇧⌘/Ctrl Z, respectively).
