# Step by Step

All settings are located in the **Select Podcast** window. It opens
automatically as soon as the widget's settings dialog appears; to close it,
click the **Select Podcast** button again.

On the left are the podcasts with their episodes; the search field above it finds
podcasts and episodes by title. In the middle, at the top, is the selected
podcast, with **Display** and **Player** below it.

## Always Show the Latest Episode

1. Open the target page for editing and drag the **Podcast Display** widget to the
   desired location.
2. In the window on the left, under **Podcasts**, click the podcast.
3. Under **Ad**, leave `Latest Episode` selected (default).
4. Under **Player**, select `Large` for wide areas or `Compact` for narrow
   columns.
5. Check the preview at the top for **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile**.
   You can further adjust the display and player to the right of the preview
   there.
6. Click **Apply** and then save the page.

From now on, the display will update automatically with each new episode.

## Display a Specific Episode

1. Open the target page for editing and drag the **Podcast Display** widget to the
   desired location.
2. In the window on the left, click the arrow next to the podcast—its episodes will
   load and appear below it.
3. Click the desired episode. The display will automatically switch to
   `Specific Episode`.
4. Under **Player**, select the size.
5. Click **Apply** and then save the page.

If the episode is missing, click **Load More Episodes** at the very bottom of the podcast list
— or type its title into the search field above the list.

## If no list appears

Instead of the list, you’ll see a message stating that the podcasts or episodes could not
be loaded. First, click **Try Again**. If that doesn’t help,
enter the information manually:

1. In the window, expand the **Enter ID Manually** section.
2. For the podcast: Open the podcast in Staffbase and copy the full
   address from the address bar.
3. For a specific episode: enter the episode ID or the full
   address of the audio file.
4. Click **Apply**. The widget will automatically retrieve the actual ID in both
   cases.

## If a podcast is missing from the list

1. Click **Can’t find the podcast?** in the lower-left corner—a panel will open on the right
   listing the common causes.
2. Check whether the podcast’s Space is accessible from this page.
   Podcasts from another Space appear in the list with the note
   “Different Space – not available here” and cannot be
   selected.
3. If necessary, enter the podcast ID manually — but then check whether the
   page’s target audience is even allowed to listen to the episode.

## Older Widgets

Widgets that were set up before the window was created will continue to display what they
previously displayed. When you open the window for the first time, it will adopt their previous
settings; only clicking **Apply** will save them in the new format.
